[U.S. Copyright Office Section 512](https://copyright.gov/512/index.html).

Georgia's consumer guidance identifies breach-notification obligations under O.C.G.A. 10-1-912. Exact coverage depends on the operator, data and incident; this review does not decide that legal classification. Include a prompt legal/incident assessment instead of promising a made-up deadline. [Georgia Attorney General guidance](https://consumered.georgia.gov/ask-ed/2023-08-30/getting-notified-following-data-breach).

California privacy coverage depends on statutory business/threshold conditions, while other jurisdictions may differ; Georgia operation and a free beta are not universal exemptions. [FTC retention/security guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business), [California Attorney General CCPA overview](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa).
